Costs: the break-even move

Every backtest here pays these. The rates in config.yaml are from memory; check your contract note.

Definition

A round trip pays brokerage, STT on the sell side, exchange and SEBI charges, GST on those fees and stamp duty on the buy side. Slippage is separate: the engine fills against you by slippage_ticks.

Why it matters

Costs are almost proportional to turnover, but price moves are measured in ticks. Small targets disappear: the table is the gross move a long needs just to get back to zero (intraday product, flat price, before slippage).

Common mistakes

  • Backtesting a 10-tick target with a Rs 80 cost per trade and no cost line.
  • Using a cost model that is out of date: STT and stamp rates have changed before.
