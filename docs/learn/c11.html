11. Delta flip, exhaustion and delta without progress

Aggression that changes sign, or that goes nowhere.

Definition

Delta flip: delta % changes sign from one bar to the next with both bars above a minimum size. Delta without progress: large one-sided delta but price barely moves in that direction (close - open no more than a few ticks). Exhaustion (aggression dries up at an extreme) needs the events module in Phase 3.

Why it matters

Progress without delta is a thin move; delta without progress hints that someone is absorbing the aggression.

How to read it

  • Orange squares: big delta, price stuck.
  • Purple dots: delta changed side.
  • Judge both against what price did next, only on confirmed bars.

Common mistakes

  • Calling every flip a reversal; in noise, delta flips constantly.
  • Ignoring that thresholds (in `config.yaml`) decide how many you see.
