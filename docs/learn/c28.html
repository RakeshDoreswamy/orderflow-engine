Event detectors and how well they were scored

Absorption, exhaustion, trapped traders, delta flip, large prints, walls, icebergs.

Definition

Each detector is a plain rule in orderflow/events.py or orderflow/book.py with thresholds in config.yaml. They only use data up to the moment they fire (prefix-tested).

Why it matters

A rule that sounds right is not a rule that works. Scoring against planted labels at least shows whether each rule finds what it claims to find.

How to read it

  • Recall: labelled events found. Precision: detections that matched a label.
  • Intervals are Wilson 95%; small samples are flagged.

Common mistakes

  • Reading these as market performance. The generator and the detectors share one author's assumptions, so this is a consistency test only.
  • Ignoring the weak ones: delta flip precision is about 0.17.
