Bridging to the ICT engine

Order-flow evidence for an ICT entry: confirm, neutral or reject.

Definition

For each entry in the ICT scanner's scan.csv the bridge sums transparent +1/-1 pieces of evidence (effort without result, flow around the swing near the sweep, net estimated delta; or absorption, exhaustion and trapped-trader events from ticks) and reports confirm, neutral, reject or no_data. Everything is cut off at the as-of time.

Does it help?

Unknown. On toy random-walk entries with no edge:

verdictnmean_Rci95small_sample
confirm1480.00-0.26 to 0.27False
neutral28040.02-0.04 to 0.08False

no_data0nannan to nanTrue
Confirm minus reject = +0.14 R, 95% CI -0.24 to +0.51: not distinguishable from zero, as it should be with no edge. Run compare_outcomes on your real ICT outcomes before trusting a verdict.
